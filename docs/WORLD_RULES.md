# MOONWITNESS — WORLD RULES

## 01 — The Moon Witnesses

The Moon is the symbolic anchor of the Witness layer.

## 02 — A Witness Records

A witness can preserve an observation without forcing a final interpretation.

## 03 — The Universe Can Stay Unresolved

Mystery, unknown, and unresolved states are valid parts of the universe.

## 04 — Identities Can Have Multiple Names

Aliases can coexist without automatically becoming separate entities.

## 05 — Past Presence Connects Eras

Something can belong to the past while still affecting the present.

## 06 — Fault Lines Reveal Pressure

A fault line marks tension or misalignment, not necessarily permanent breakage.

## 07 — Promoting Moves Forward

Promoting asks what deserves to continue into the next phase.

## 08 — Narrative Roles Stay Contextual

Villain, Dreamer, Runaway, Seduction, Fault Line, and Sanity's Last Breath are story roles or narrative constructs.

## 09 — The Story Can Evolve

Canon may grow. Lore may remain unresolved.

## 10 — Keep It Simple

World rules should make creation and exploration easier, not harder.
