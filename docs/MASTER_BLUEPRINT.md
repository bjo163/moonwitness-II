## 04 — CORE FUNCTIONS

1. **Storytelling** — turn people, events, memories, fragments, and moments into stories.
2. **Exposure** — make hidden signals and connections visible.
3. **Change Advisory Board** — observe transitions, consequences, decisions, and what comes next.
4. **Chasing Heart** — pursue longing, attachment, and connection.
5. **Lovestruck** — love, vulnerability, attraction, romance, and emotional tension.
6. **Breath Electric** — energy, atmosphere, movement, rhythm, and emotional electricity.
7. **Seduction** — attraction, curiosity, temptation, fascination, desire, and attachment.
8. **Fault Lines** — detect and visualize pressure, misalignment, fracture, instability, and transition.
9. **Villain of the Story** — introduce disruption, temptation, opposition, conflict, revelation, and transformation as narrative forces.
10. **Witness Archive** — preserve events, observations, messages, memories, relationships, and changes.
11. **Sanity's Last Breath** — the final narrative threshold before surrender, silence, escape, revelation, transformation, or the end of a phase.
12. **Past Presence** — explore how the past remains present through memory, trace, influence, echo, absence, objects, places, messages, relationships, habits, and signals.
13. **Promoting** — identify what from the past deserves to be carried forward into the next phase.
