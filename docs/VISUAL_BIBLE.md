## Meaning

**Lines** — connection, direction, separation, movement, memory, witness.

**Fault Lines** — broken lines, splits, cracks, offsets, glitches, discontinuity.

**Sanity's Last Breath** — fading signal, flicker, breath pulse, distortion, silence, blackout.

**Past Presence** — echo, afterimage, residual light, repeated pattern, shadow.

**Promoting** — rising line, forward light, continuity, carrying a signal onward.

## Principle

Visuals should support discovery and atmosphere without becoming visual bureaucracy.
