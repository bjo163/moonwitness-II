# MOONWITNESS — CANON

## Purpose

Canon keeps the universe coherent without freezing it.

## Levels

### CANON
Established inside the MoonWitness universe.

### LORE
Belongs to the universe but may remain unresolved.

### INTERPRETATION
A theory, reading, perspective, or observation.

### UNKNOWN
Not established yet.

## Rules

- Interpretation does not automatically become canon.
- Lore can evolve into canon.
- An unresolved item may remain unresolved.
- A name or alias does not automatically identify a distinct person.
- A witness record does not automatically establish objective truth.
- Narrative roles describe story context.

## Simple Transition

```
IDEA
 ↓
LORE
 ↓
CANON
```

Or remain:

```
IDEA → UNKNOWN
IDEA → INTERPRETATION
LORE → UNRESOLVED
```

## Principle

> Keep the universe open.
> Keep the structure clear.
